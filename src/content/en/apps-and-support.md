Discover a suite of applications designed to improve your digital experience. Each app is built with attention to detail, performance, and user privacy in mind.

## Our Apps

We develop software that prioritizes:

- **User privacy:** Your data stays yours. We do not track users for advertising or sell personal data.
- **Performance:** Applications are optimized for speed and efficiency on supported devices.
- **Design:** Interfaces are designed to be clear and intuitive.
- **Accessibility:** We aim to make our applications usable with screen readers and assistive technologies.

## Support

For help with an application, contact [support@lbenicio.dev](mailto:support@lbenicio.dev). Feedback and feature requests are welcome. Documentation is available within the relevant applications.

## Privacy

Our applications aim to collect only the data needed to provide their features, store data locally when practical, and protect cloud-synced information with encryption where supported. We do not sell personal information.

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://legal.lbenicio.dev/docs/privacy-policy) for details about data handling.
