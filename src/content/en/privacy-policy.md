## Introduction

Hello! I'm **Leonardo Benicio**, an independent software developer based in Brazil. I develop web applications, macOS apps, iOS apps, and provide consulting services as a solo developer ("Indie Developer").

This Privacy Policy explains how I handle information when you use my software, websites, or services. As an indie developer, I value transparency and privacy, and I've designed my systems with these principles in mind.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- My websites and web applications (including future projects)
- macOS applications I develop and distribute
- iOS applications I develop and distribute
- Any consulting or development services I provide
- Communications with me (email, support requests, etc.)

## What Information I Collect

### 1. Technical & Diagnostic Data (Self-Hosted Analytics)
I collect minimal technical information to improve my applications:

- **App Usage Data:** Basic usage statistics (features used, session duration)
- **Performance Metrics:** App performance, loading times, resource usage
- **Crash Reports:** Anonymous crash information to fix bugs
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, language settings
- **Technical Logs:** Error logs and diagnostic information

**Important:** This data is:
- **Anonymous:** Not linked to your personal identity
- **Self-Hosted:** Stored on my own servers, not third-party analytics services
- **Diagnostic-Only:** Used solely to improve app stability and performance

### 2. Information You Provide Directly
- **Support Requests:** Information you provide when contacting me for support
- **Feedback:** Suggestions, bug reports, or feature requests
- **Consulting Inquiries:** Information shared when discussing potential projects

### 3. Website Usage Data
My websites may collect basic access logs, such as the requested page and browser information. These Hugo sites use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a service provided by Google, to measure website usage: [lbenicio.dev](https://lbenicio.dev), [blog.lbenicio.dev](https://blog.lbenicio.dev), [apps.lbenicio.dev](https://apps.lbenicio.dev), [socials.lbenicio.dev](https://socials.lbenicio.dev), and [newsletter.lbenicio.dev](https://newsletter.lbenicio.dev). Their tags are enabled on production pages and configured to honor a browser's Do Not Track signal.

Those same Hugo sites also configure the Google Ads tag (AW-18594527327) for advertising measurement. Google explains that its conversion measurement may use cookies and ad-click identifiers; see Google's [conversion measurement documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7521212?hl=en). The tag is enabled on production pages and configured to honor a browser's Do Not Track signal.

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and process pseudonymous identifiers, page interactions, browser and device details, and approximate location. Google explains its data collection and processing in its [Analytics data safeguards](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Other websites and applications may use self-hosted analytics or no analytics, depending on the service.

## What I Don't Collect

- **Personal Identifiable Information:** I don't collect names, email addresses, or personal details unless you explicitly provide them for support
- **Location Data:** I don't track your precise location
- **Contact Lists:** I don't access your contacts, messages, or personal files
- **Financial Information:** I don't collect payment details (handled by app stores or payment processors)
- **User Content:** I don't access your personal data within apps unless necessary for specific app functionality (and then only with explicit permission)

## How I Use Collected Information

1. **Improve Software:** Fix bugs, optimize performance, and enhance user experience
2. **Diagnose Issues:** Understand and resolve technical problems
3. **Plan Development:** Determine which features to build or improve based on usage patterns
4. **Communicate:** Respond to support requests and feedback
5. **Legal Compliance:** Fulfill legal obligations if required

## Data Storage & Security

- **Self-Hosted Infrastructure:** All data is stored on servers I control or on reputable cloud providers with strong privacy commitments
- **Data Retention:** Diagnostic data is typically retained for 90 days, then automatically deleted
- **Security Measures:** I implement appropriate technical measures to protect data, though no system is 100% secure
- **Backups:** Regular backups are performed for disaster recovery

## Data Sharing & Third Parties

**I do not sell, rent, or trade your data.**

The only exceptions:

1. **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect my rights
2. **Service Providers:** Infrastructure providers (hosting, email) who process data on my behalf under strict confidentiality agreements
3. **Analytics Providers:** Google processes usage data for the websites listed above under its applicable terms and privacy policies. Other service providers may process data as described above.
4. **App Stores:** Standard analytics provided by Apple App Store or similar platforms (subject to their respective privacy policies)
